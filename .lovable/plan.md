

# 💝 Valentine's Day Surprise for Your Girlfriend

## Overview
A romantic, soft & elegant website that opens with a "tap to begin" interaction, then launches into a dreamy experience with background music, animated effects, a typing love message, and an endless slideshow of your couple photos.

---

## 1. Welcome / Entry Screen
- A beautiful full-screen landing with a soft pink/blush gradient background
- Elegant cursive text: **"I made this for you 💕"**
- A glowing **"Tap to Begin"** button with a gentle pulse animation
- This first interaction allows the browser to play background music

## 2. Background Music
- Your chosen song plays on loop after the user taps "Begin"
- A small, subtle music toggle button (mute/unmute) in the corner so she can control it

## 3. Romantic Animation Effects
- **Falling hearts** — soft, semi-transparent hearts gently floating down the screen
- **Rose petals** — delicate petals drifting and rotating as they fall
- **Floating particles** — sparkly glowing dots creating a dreamy atmosphere
- All animations run continuously in the background, layered behind the content

## 4. Love Message with Typing Animation
- After tapping "Begin", a heartfelt message appears letter by letter in elegant cursive font
- You'll customize the text (e.g., "Happy Valentine's Day, my love...")
- The message fades in beautifully before the slideshow begins

## 5. Endless Photo Slideshow
- Your couple photos displayed in a beautiful frame with soft rounded corners and a subtle glow
- Photos crossfade smoothly into each other on an endless loop
- Each photo stays visible for ~4-5 seconds before transitioning

## 6. Design & Color Palette
- **Soft & Elegant** theme: pastel pinks, blush tones, warm whites
- Cursive/script fonts for headings, clean serif for body text
- Subtle gradient backgrounds with a dreamy, romantic feel
- Everything responsive so it looks great on her phone too

---

## What You'll Need to Upload
- 📸 Your couple photos (as many as you'd like, 10+)
- 🎵 A background song (MP3 file)
- 💌 Your personal love message text
